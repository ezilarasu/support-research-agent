# Billing Policies & Refund Guidelines

## Refund Policy

### Standard Refunds (under $500)
Refunds under $500 are processed automatically within 3–5 business days.
No manager approval is required for standard refunds.
The customer receives an email confirmation when the refund is initiated.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

### Large Refunds ($500 and above)
Refunds of $500 or more require manager approval before processing.
The support agent must submit an approval request with:
- Customer account ID
- Original transaction ID
- Refund amount and reason
- Customer's stated reason for the refund

Approval typically takes 1 business day. The customer is notified once approval is granted.

## Double Charge Resolution
If a customer was charged twice for the same subscription:
1. Verify both charges in the billing system
2. Issue a refund for the duplicate charge immediately (no approval required)
3. Note the incident in the customer record
4. Send a follow-up apology email

Double charges under $200 can be refunded same-day.

## Subscription Cancellation and Proration
- Customers can cancel at any time
- No refund is issued for the current billing period
- Access continues until the end of the paid period
- Annual subscribers: prorated refund available within 30 days of renewal

## Disputed Charges
For disputed charges (charge not recognised by customer):
1. Ask for the last 4 digits of the card
2. Verify the charge matches a legitimate order
3. If no match: initiate chargeback dispute process
4. If legitimate but forgotten: remind customer of the purchase

## Invoice Corrections
Incorrect invoices can be corrected and reissued within 60 days of the original issue date.
Tax corrections require finance team review.

## Payment Failure
If a payment fails:
- Automatic retry: 3 attempts over 7 days
- Customer notification: email on each failed attempt
- Account suspension: after 3 failed attempts, access is limited
- Reactivation: once payment is updated and succeeds

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
A: Standard refunds (under $500) take 3–5 business days. Large refunds take up to 2 business days after approval.

**Q: Can I get a refund after 30 days?**
A: Standard policy covers refunds within 30 days. Exceptions can be requested through the billing team for compelling reasons.

**Q: Why was I charged twice?**
A: Double charges can occur due to payment processing errors. We always refund the duplicate charge immediately.

**Q: Can I change my billing date?**
A: Yes, billing dates can be adjusted once per year. Contact support to request this change.
